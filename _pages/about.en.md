I'm a data scientist and economist with a background in applied statistics. I specialize in turning scattered data into reliable measurements and models that support decisions, in both the public and private sectors.

In the **public sector**, I designed methodologies and calculation processes for official statistics, such as the Gross Geographic Product estimates for the statistics offices of Corrientes and Chaco, Argentina. In the **private sector**, I built geospatial machine learning models for automated real estate valuation, and cloud-based measurement systems that reach the client as easy-to-read dashboards. I also teach Econometrics at Universidad Nacional del Nordeste.

I care about rigor and reproducibility: every analysis is documented, version-controlled and ready to be updated. You can see some of this work in [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}).
